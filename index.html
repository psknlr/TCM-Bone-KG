Reading the classics of bone, with evidence you can weigh. 以可称量的证据，
重读骨之古典。

TCM-Bone-KG is an evidence-weighted knowledge graph of osteoporosis-related bone disorders (骨痿, 骨枯, 骨痹, 骨空 …) distilled from 516 classical Chinese medical texts spanning ten dynasties. Every relation carries passage-level provenance and a Triple Evidence Confidence score; a strictly non-diagnostic bridge connects classical concepts to modern clinical anchors. TCM-Bone-KG 是一个证据加权的中医骨病知识图谱，从横跨十个朝代的 516 部中医古籍中提炼出骨痿、骨枯、骨痹、骨空等与骨质疏松相关的病证体系。每一条关系都带有条目级溯源与「三元组证据置信度」评分；一层严格非诊断性的桥接层将古典概念与现代临床锚点相连。

Overview概览

Three ideas hold the graph together支撑图谱的三个核心设计

The graph is not a list of extracted facts. It is a provenance-bearing, evidence-graded, boundary-aware representation of how classical physicians wrote about bone. 图谱并非抽取事实的简单堆叠，而是一种携带溯源、分级证据、明确边界的表示方式，记录古代医家如何论述「骨」。

Evidence-weighted, not merely extracted证据加权，而非简单抽取

Every evidence triple carries a Triple Evidence Confidence (TEC) score on a single [0, 1] scale, combining passage relevance with how often it recurs, across how many independent books, and how widely across dynasties. Rankings are stable to ±20% weight perturbation (ρ ≥ 0.998). 每条证据三元组都拥有统一 [0,1] 尺度的三元组证据置信度（TEC），综合条目相关度、出现频次、独立书目数与朝代跨度。在 ±20% 权重扰动下排序相关系数 ρ ≥ 0.998，结果稳健。

An orthogonal modern bridge正交的现代临床桥接层

Twelve modern clinical anchors (disease context, population context, fragility/functional phenotype, assessment) sit beside the seven-layer classical ontology, never inside it. Classical concepts connect only through non-diagnostic correspondence relations. 12 个现代临床锚点（疾病情境、人群情境、脆性/功能表型、评估工具）与七层古典本体并置，而非作为第八层嵌入。古典概念只能通过非诊断性的对应关系与之相连。

A formal diagnostic boundary形式化的诊断边界

No classical text records DXA or a T-score. A binary gate G(c,m) therefore forces CMSexact = 0 for every disease/syndrome → Osteoporosis pair: 100% compliance, encoded in the data, so the graph cannot be read as retrospective diagnosis. 古籍中不存在 DXA 或 T 值记录。二值门控 G(c,m) 强制所有病名/证型 → 骨质疏松症的 CMSexact = 0，合规率 100%，从数据层面杜绝将图谱解读为回溯性诊断。

Architecture图谱架构

Seven classical layers, one orthogonal bridge七层古典本体 · 一层正交桥接

The ontology follows the clinical reasoning path of the classics, from disease name to outcome. The modern layer is deliberately drawn beside it.本体沿古籍临床推理路径展开：由病名至转归。现代层被有意绘制于其侧，而非其上。

  • L1–L2Disease & syndrome病名与证型11 classical bone-disease names (骨痿, 骨枯, 骨痹 …) and 7 syndrome patterns such as liver–kidney yin deficiency and kidney yang deficiency.11 个古籍骨病病名（骨痿、骨枯、骨痹……）与 7 个证型，如肝肾阴虚证、肾阳虚证。
  • L3–L4Phenotype & population表型与人群8 observable phenotypes (functional limitation, bone pain, kyphosis, height loss …) and 4 population contexts (elderly, perimenopause, pregnancy/lactation, emotional disturbance).8 个可观察表型（活动受限、骨痛、驼背、身高变矮……）与 4 类人群情境（老年后、围绝经期、胎产哺乳、情志异常）。
  • L5–L6Therapy & materia medica治法与方药4 therapeutic methods, 508 formulas, 369 herbs in 13 functional categories, 161 acupoints on 15 meridians, and 48 external agents.4 种治法、508 首方剂、13 类功效下的 369 味中药、15 条经络上的 161 个腧穴，以及 48 种外治材料。
  • L7Outcome转归Recorded outcomes: cured, improved, unhealed, died.记载的转归：痊愈、好转、未愈、死亡。
  • MModern clinical anchors现代临床锚点12 anchors in 4 categories. Assessment anchors (DXA/BMD, T-score, fracture-risk and vertebral-fracture assessment) are reachable only from within the modern layer via guideline-derived structural edges.4 类共 12 个锚点。评估类锚点（DXA/BMD、T 值、骨折风险评估、椎体骨折评估）仅能通过指南来源的结构性关系从现代层内部到达。

Methods方法

How evidence becomes weight证据如何转化为权重

Three boxed definitions, exactly as used to build the released graph. Full text in docs/methods_results_draft.md.以下三个定义框与发布图谱的构建过程完全一致。完整文本见 docs/methods_results_draft.md。

§2.9 Triple Evidence Confidence (TEC)§2.9 三元组证据置信度（TEC）

For a triple t = (subject, relation, object), TEC combines the mean upstream relevance of its supporting passages with three corroboration signals: recurrence, independent books, and dynastic spread.对于三元组 t =（主语，关系，宾语），TEC 将支持条目的平均相关度与三种佐证信号结合：复现次数、独立书目数、朝代离散度。

Box 1 · Triple Evidence Confidence定义框 1 · 三元组证据置信度
St = log(1+nt) / log(1+nmax)
Bt = log(1+bt) / log(1+bmax)
Dt = H(dynasty distribution of t) / log(K), K = 10
TECt = R̄t · (α·St + β·Bt + γ·Dt), α = β = γ = 1/3
nt
supporting passages (nmax = 400)支持条目数（nmax = 400）
bt
distinct books (bmax = 152)独立书目数（bmax = 152）
R̄t
mean per-passage osteoporosis-relevance score ∈ [0,1]条目级骨质疏松相关度均值 ∈ [0,1]
Dt
normalised Shannon entropy of dynasty labels朝代标签的归一化香农熵

Edges are partitioned into evidence (9,201; TEC computed), structural (1,207; curated facts, TEC undefined) and bridge-score (26; weighted by CMS). Evidence edges are tiered E1–E4 by breadth of attestation.边划分为证据类（9,201 条，计算 TEC）、结构类（1,207 条，策展事实，TEC 未定义）与桥接评分类（26 条，以 CMS 加权）。证据类边依据佐证广度分为 E1–E4 四级。

§2.11 Cross-era phenotype bridging (ECPB)§2.11 跨时代表型桥接（ECPB）

Each of 18 classical concepts gets a TEC-weighted phenotype profile xc over 8 phenotypes and a population profile uc over 4 contexts. Each modern anchor gets clinical-judgment vectors ym, um on a {0, 0.5, 1} scale.18 个古典概念各自获得 TEC 加权的 8 维表型谱 xc 与 4 维人群谱 uc；每个现代锚点则依据临床判断给定 {0, 0.5, 1} 尺度的向量 ym、um。

Box 2 · PHS, PCS and the Cross-Era Mapping Score定义框 2 · PHS、PCS 与跨时代映射分数
PHS(c,m) = Σk ωk·min(xck, ymk) / Σk ωk·max(xck, ymk)
PCS(c,m) = cosine(uc, um)
Ec = mean TEC over evidence edges touching c
CMS(c,m) = Ec · (λ·PHS + (1−λ)·PCS), λ = 0.5
Box 3 · Diagnostic Boundary Constraint定义框 3 · 诊断边界约束
G(c,m) = 0 if m = Osteoporosis read diagnostically
G(c,m) = 1 for phenotype / population correspondence
CMSexact(c,m) = G(c,m) · CMS(c,m)

All 21 disease/syndrome → Osteoporosis edges carry CMSexact = 0 by construction.全部 21 条病名/证型 → 骨质疏松症的边，其 CMSexact 依构造恒为 0。

          How to read the bridge如何阅读桥接层

          • Classical disease names are not retrospective diagnoses of osteoporosis.古籍病名不是骨质疏松症的回溯性诊断。
          • CMS expresses phenotype and population-context similarity, scaled by evidence strength Ec.CMS 表达的是表型与人群情境的相似度，并按证据强度 Ec 缩放。
          • The anchor vectors ym, um are a modelling choice awaiting domain-expert review; treat specific CMS values accordingly.锚点向量 ym、um 是等待领域专家审阅的建模选择；具体 CMS 数值应据此谨慎对待。
          • Assessment anchors (DXA/BMD, T-score) are reachable only through guideline-derived structural edges within the modern layer.评估类锚点（DXA/BMD、T 值）仅能通过现代层内部的指南来源结构性关系到达。
